ABOUT NYC FIRESTOPPING

Protecting New York City, One Building at a Time

NYC Firestopping is a New York City–based fire protection company specializing in firestopping, fireproofing, and fire protection consultation for construction projects across all five boroughs.

We focus on protecting buildings by addressing areas where fire and smoke can potentially spread, including penetrations, joints, and other openings in fire-resistance-rated construction. Our certified team brings years of experience to every project, ensuring compliance with NYC Department of Buildings requirements.

WHAT WE DO

Our Fire Protection Services

Comprehensive fire safety solutions for every type of construction project.

Firestopping

Preventing the passage of fire and smoke through penetrations, joints, and openings in fire-resistance-rated walls, floors, and assemblies.

  • Mechanical penetrations
  • Electrical penetrations
  • Pipes & conduits
  • Wall & floor penetrations
  • Construction joints
  • Fire-rated assemblies
Learn More →

Fireproofing

Applying fire-resistant materials to structural elements and building components to increase their resistance to fire.

  • Structural steel
  • Columns & beams
  • Structural elements
  • Fire-resistant coatings
  • Building components
